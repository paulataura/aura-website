Understand the asset.

Reliable information makes practical upkeep possible. ASG brings intelligent monitoring, planned maintenance and response into one programme, shaped around the assets and the site they serve.

We review the assets, their service history and the signals available. Telemetry works alongside inspections and maintenance records to show what needs attention. Monitoring informs the work; it does not replace visits and servicing.

Questions before you enquire

Smart telemetry and monitoring explained.

Can ASG manage existing assets?

Yes, we welcome enquiries about existing domestic and commercial assets. Share equipment and telemetry details so we can review the support needed.

Can you work with our current telemetry?

We will review the provider, access arrangements and available signals before agreeing a monitoring service. Compatibility and responsibilities depend on the system.

How often should the station be serviced?

The schedule depends on equipment requirements, usage, condition and past issues. We agree the programme after reviewing the station rather than applying one interval to every site.

What if an asset reports a fault?

A stopped pump or changing level may be normal or may need investigation. Signals, readings and the operating arrangement help distinguish normal operation from an issue.

How do I contact ASG in an emergency?

Call 01386 571556 for our 24/7 emergency contact. Tell us the site location and what the system is reporting so we can assess the issue and discuss response arrangements.
