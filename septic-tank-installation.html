The right arrangement starts with the site.

A septic tank is one part of the drainage arrangement. The property use, ground conditions, available space and discharge route all need attention when assessing an installation or replacement.

We discuss your existing system, the proposed work and the practical requirements for excavation, access and connections. Where a sewage treatment plant may be more appropriate, that should be considered before deciding on equipment.

Planning your enquiry

Useful details help us scope the work.

Send your postcode, the number and type of properties served, and whether this is a new installation or a replacement. Existing drawings, tank information and details of the current discharge arrangement are useful.

Tell us about access, available space and any changes to the property. We can then discuss the site review and information needed to establish the next step.

For small sewage discharges in England, the applicable general binding rules or permit requirements depend on the arrangement. Review the official septic tank and treatment plant guidance when planning work; installation alone does not establish that a discharge is permitted.

Common questions

Before you get in touch.

Do you replace existing septic tanks?

We welcome replacement enquiries. Share the current tank, discharge arrangement, service history and reason for replacement so we can discuss a suitable scope.

Is a septic tank always the right choice?

No. Suitability depends on the site and discharge arrangement. A sewage treatment system or another drainage solution may need consideration.

Can you include the drainage and civils work?

Yes. Associated drainage and groundwork can be discussed as part of the installation rather than as separate unrelated jobs.

Can you help with ongoing care?

We can discuss a programme covering inspection, maintenance and relevant collections, tailored to the installed system and the site.
