Common questions

Do you offer emergency tankers?

Yes, 24/7. Call 01386 571556.

How often should my septic tank be emptied?

Usually at least once a year, but it depends on the tank size and how many people use it. We can set up reminders.

Can you help when a pump station fails?

Yes. We can pump out the wet well to prevent an overflow while the fault is fixed.
