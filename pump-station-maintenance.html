Pump station maintenance.

Planned servicing, wet well cleans and fast fault response, so your pump station keeps running and problems are caught early.

Talk to an expertCall 01386 571556 ↗

Keep the station ready, not just running.

Pump stations fail when small things go unnoticed: a sticking float, a worn impeller, fat building up in the wet well. Regular servicing catches them before they become an overflow or an emergency.

We look after domestic, commercial and adoptable stations, and can add telemetry where it helps you see what's happening between visits.

  • Planned servicing of pumps and controls
  • Wet well cleaning and desilting
  • Float and alarm checks
  • Fault finding and repairs
  • Emergency tankering if a station fails
  • Clear service reports

What a service covers

Pumps & controls

Checking pump operation, duty and standby, control panels and kiosks.

Wet well

Inspecting and cleaning the wet well, floats and level controls.

Report & advise

Common questions

How often should a pump station be serviced?

It depends on the station and how it's used. Most need servicing at least once or twice a year, and busier or adoptable stations more often.

Can you take over an existing pump station?

Yes. We'll review its condition and history first and tell you honestly what needs doing.

Can you work with our current telemetry?

Often, yes. Tell us what's installed and we'll check.

What if a pump stops at the weekend?

Call our 24/7 line on 01386 571556. We'll arrange attendance and emergency tankering if needed.
