Why servicing matters

Keep the station ready, not just running.

Pump stations fail when small things go unnoticed: a sticking float, a worn impeller, fat building up in the wet well. Regular servicing catches them before they become an overflow or an emergency.

We look after domestic, commercial and adoptable stations.

  • Planned servicing of pumps and controls
  • Wet well cleaning and desilting
  • Float and alarm checks
  • Fault finding and repairs
  • Emergency tankering if a station fails
  • Clear service reports

How a pump station works

What we check, and why it matters.

Every point below can stop a station working. A proper service checks them all.

  1. 1

    Control panel & kiosk

    Starts and stops the pumps and swaps duty and standby. We check settings, alarms and wiring.

  2. 2

    Telemetry

    Sends alarms and performance data so problems are spotted between visits.

  3. 3

    Inlet

    Where sewage arrives from the site. We look for blockages, debris and signs of ingress.

  4. 4

    Wet well

    Holds the incoming flow. We clean out fat, silt and rags that cause pumps to block.

  5. 5

    Duty & standby pumps

    Two pumps share the work so one can take over if the other fails. We check wear, blockages and running.

  6. 6

    Level floats & high-level alarm

    Tell the panel when to start and stop, and raise the alarm if the level gets too high. We test every one.

  7. 7

    Rising main

    Carries the flow away under pressure. We check valves and look for signs of restriction.

Common questions

How often should a pump station be serviced?

It depends on the station and how it's used. Most need servicing at least once or twice a year, and busier or adoptable stations more often.

Can you take over an existing pump station?

Yes. We'll review its condition and history first and tell you honestly what needs doing.

Can you work with our current telemetry?

Often, yes. Tell us what's installed and we'll check.

What if a pump stops at the weekend?

Call our 24/7 line on 01386 571556. We'll arrange attendance and emergency tankering if needed.
