Intelligent monitoring

Telemetry turns information into action.

Our own telemetry system can collect levels, pump states, floats, faults, flow and other available performance signals from tanks, wet wells, pump stations and related assets.

We use the data to understand the system, identify changes early and coordinate the right response: planned maintenance, an engineer, cleaning, a tanker or remedial work. Monitoring works alongside site visits and servicing, giving the customer one team responsible for what happens next.
