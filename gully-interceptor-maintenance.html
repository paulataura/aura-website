Common questions

Gully and interceptor maintenance explained.

Can this be part of a maintenance contract?

Yes. Gullies and interceptors can sit within a wider drainage asset management programme alongside monitoring, pump station upkeep and scheduled collections.

How often do they need attention?

Frequency depends on the asset, site activity, material build-up and previous findings. We review those factors before agreeing a schedule.

Can you collect interceptor waste?

Send details of the contents, capacity and any previous waste information. Collection and acceptance arrangements are assessed for the particular stream, including potentially hazardous material.

Can you help with new installations?

Discuss gully or interceptor installation through our drainage and sewage installation service, which includes domestic and commercial civils work.
