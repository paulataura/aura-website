Jetting & drain cleaning.

High-pressure jetting to clear blockages, fat, silt and debris from drains, sewers and culverts.

Clear it, then find out why.

Jetting clears most blockages quickly. But if the same drain keeps blocking, there's usually a reason, so we'll tell you what we think is causing it and how to stop it happening again.

We can pair jetting with a CCTV survey to see the condition of the drain once it's clear.

  • Blocked drains and sewers
  • Fat, oil and grease build-up
  • Silt and debris
  • Culverts and larger pipes
  • Planned cleaning programmes
  • Emergency call-outs

Common questions

Can you come out the same day?

Often, yes. For emergencies call 01386 571556.

Will jetting damage my drains?

Jetting is safe for drains in reasonable condition. If we suspect damage, we'll suggest a CCTV survey first.

Can you set up regular cleaning?

Yes, for drains prone to fat or silt build-up we can schedule regular jetting.
