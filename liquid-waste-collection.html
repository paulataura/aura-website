Liquid waste requirements vary with the material produced, how it is stored and how often it needs removal. We welcome enquiries for one-off requirements and scheduled collections connected with ongoing site management.

Tell us about the stream before arranging a collection. Tank contents, interceptor waste, process liquids and wash water may need different assessment and acceptance arrangements. Potentially hazardous liquids are reviewed individually.

Planning your enquiry

Useful details help us scope the work.

Start with the site postcode, a description of the material and the estimated quantity. Include how it is stored, vehicle access, collection point details and any previous waste documentation.

For a process liquid, include available composition, classification or safety information. If the contents are uncertain, say so: further assessment may be needed before acceptance can be confirmed.

We discuss the collection requirement and arrangements after reviewing those details. Regular collections can be considered within a wider programme for interceptors, pump stations and drainage assets.

Common questions

Before you get in touch.

Can I arrange regular collections?

Yes. Tell us the material and typical volume or frequency so we can discuss a schedule that fits the operation.

Can you collect interceptor waste?

We welcome enquiries. Share the interceptor type, contents, volume and previous documentation so collection and acceptance arrangements can be assessed.

What about hazardous liquids?

Use our hazardous liquid waste enquiry page or call with the details. Acceptance is case by case; further information may be required.

Do you handle general refuse or skip waste?

No. ASG’s focus here is food waste, wastewater and liquid waste requirements. This collection page covers liquid streams.
