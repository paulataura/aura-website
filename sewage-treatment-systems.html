Plan the system and its ongoing care.

A treatment system must suit the site it serves. Occupancy, use, existing drainage, discharge arrangements and access affect the requirements. We review those details with you rather than treating the installation as an equipment-only purchase.

ASG welcomes enquiries about new systems and existing installations that need attention. We can discuss associated groundworks and pipework, servicing needs and how the system fits with the wider drainage assets.

Talk about your treatment system

Planning your enquiry

Useful details help us scope the work.

For an installation enquiry, send the postcode, intended use and details of any current system. For maintenance, include the manufacturer or model where known, service history and any reported issues.

We discuss the practical scope, access and follow-up information needed. Ongoing servicing and monitoring arrangements should reflect the system, its use and the manufacturer’s requirements.

Discharge requirements must be considered alongside installation. The official England guidance for septic tanks and treatment plants explains when the general binding rules or an environmental permit apply.

Common questions

Before you get in touch.

Do you work with domestic and commercial systems?

Yes. Tell us the premises type, system details and what you need so we can review the particular requirement.

Can you maintain an existing treatment plant?

We welcome existing-system enquiries. The equipment, access, service history and support requirements need review before agreeing the programme.

Can treatment and pump station maintenance be combined?

Yes. A drainage asset management plan can coordinate treatment system care with pump stations, telemetry and other site assets.

What should I do if the system reports a fault?

Call our 24/7 emergency contact on 01386 571556. Share the site location, equipment and reported fault so response arrangements can be discussed.
