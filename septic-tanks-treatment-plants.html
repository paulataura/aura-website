The right system for your property.

Not every property suits a septic tank. Where the system discharges, how many people it serves and the ground conditions all affect what's right, and what the rules allow.

We look at all of it first, recommend the right option, and take care of the installation from start to finish.

  • New septic tanks and treatment plants
  • Replacement of old or failing systems
  • Upgrades where rules require it
  • Drainage fields and connections
  • Servicing and emptying after install
  • Homes, farms and businesses

Common questions

Is a septic tank always the right choice?

No. Depending on where it discharges and the site, a sewage treatment plant may be needed instead. We'll tell you honestly which suits your property.

Can you replace my existing septic tank?

Yes. We remove or decommission the old system and install the right replacement.

Do you include the drainage work?

Yes. We can handle the connections and drainage field as part of the job.

Can you service and empty it afterwards?

Yes. We can set up planned servicing and emptying so you don't have to remember.
