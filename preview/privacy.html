When you send an enquiry we collect the details you give us: your name, phone number, email address, site postcode and a description of what you need.

We use your details to respond to your enquiry and, if you go ahead, to provide our services. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.

We don't sell your information. We share it only with service providers who help us run our business, such as our website and database hosting, and with contractors we arrange to carry out work you've asked for.

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and for a reasonable period afterwards. If you become a customer, we keep records for as long as required for legal and accounting purposes.
