Pump station installation.

Package and adoptable pumping stations designed, installed and commissioned properly, with a clear route to adoption where it's needed.

From design to commissioning.

A pump station has to suit the flows, the site and, on new developments, the water company's adoption standards. Getting that right at design stage saves months of problems later.

We keep projects moving from first drawings through installation and commissioning, then support the station until it's adopted or hand it into our care.

  • Domestic and commercial package stations
  • Adoptable pumping stations for developers
  • Wet wells, controls and kiosks
  • Commissioning and handover
  • Telemetry where it helps
  • Support through to adoption

What we do

Design support

Working with your designers to get a station that suits the site and adoption standards.

Installation

Installing the station, wet well, pipework, controls and kiosk.

Commissioning & handover

Testing, commissioning and the records needed for adoption or handover.

Common questions

Do you install adoptable pumping stations?

Yes. We install stations intended for adoption under a Section 104 agreement and support the process through to adoption.

Can you maintain the station before adoption?

Yes. Most stations need maintaining between commissioning and adoption, and we can look after that for you.

Do you install pump stations for single homes?

Yes, we install package pump stations for private properties as well as developments.

Can you add telemetry?

Yes, where it helps. It gives early warning of faults and useful data during the maintenance period.
