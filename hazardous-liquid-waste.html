A service shaped around the site

Start with the material, not a blanket promise.

A process liquid, contaminated wash water or tank residue needs a clear description before a collection route can be considered. The source of the waste, its composition and any existing classification information help us understand the requirement.

We welcome enquiries without suggesting every material can be accepted. Where information is incomplete, further assessment or documentation may be needed before arrangements can be confirmed.

Planning your enquiry

Useful details help us scope the work.

Send the site postcode, contact details, material description and estimated quantity. State whether the contents are mixed, how they are stored and whether the composition has changed since previous collections.

Include any existing waste classification and analysis. A product safety data sheet can be useful background but should not be treated as a complete waste classification on its own.

Our initial discussion establishes what is known, what further information is required and whether suitable acceptance and collection arrangements can be considered. For classification responsibilities, consult the official waste classification guidance.

Common questions

Before you get in touch.

Do you accept every hazardous liquid?

No. Each enquiry is assessed by material, information available and suitable acceptance arrangements. Contact us with the details before assuming a stream can be collected.

What if I do not know the composition?

Say what is known about the source and contents. Further information or assessment may be necessary before any arrangement can be confirmed.

Can this be part of a regular collection plan?

Discuss the stream and frequency with us. Any ongoing arrangements need to reflect the assessed material and changes to its composition.

How do I discuss an urgent requirement?

Call 01386 571556 using our 24/7 emergency contact. Describe the issue and location so we can discuss appropriate next steps.
